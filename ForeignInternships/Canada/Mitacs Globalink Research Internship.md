- _Note for Indian Applicants:_ Eligibility criteria and eligible fields for India can vary by agreement cycle. Check the country-specific portal for the current cycle.

### General Rankings

- **Mitacs Ranking:** Mitacs is an officially recognized, highly prestigious government-and-partner-backed Canadian non-profit. It holds an equivalent standing to programs like Germany’s **DAAD WISE** or France’s **Charpak**.
    
- **Host University Rankings:** Host institutions range from top global research universities (University of Toronto, McGill, UBC, Waterloo) to specialized regional universities across all Canadian provinces.

### Main Domains

Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Electrical, Mechanical, Aerospace, Bio-engineering, Data Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, and Mathematics

#### Other domains

including Humanities, Social Sciences, Economics, Business, Environmental Studies, and Fine Arts

### Details of Exchange program

#### Duration

- **Total Length:** **12 consecutive weeks**.
- **Window:** Occurs between **May 1 and October 31**. Your start date must fall between **May 1 and July 31**.

#### Nomination Process

- **Direct Application:** You apply directly via the Mitacs Globalink portal during your 3rd year.
    
- **Institutional Approval / Verification:** Depending on country-specific agreements (e.g., AICTE/IIT partnerships in India), your home institute verifies your enrollment and academic standing after submission.

#### Fees/Stipend and Expenses Management

- **Round-trip Flight:** Stipend provided for travel to and from Canada.
    
- **Living Allowance:** Direct monthly stipend to cover food, daily expenses, and local transportation.
    
- **Student Enrollment Fees:** Covered at the host Canadian university.
    
- **Health Insurance:** Emergency medical insurance is fully provided for the 12-week duration.
    
- **Funding Delivery:** Stipends are released in installments upon your arrival and setup of a Canadian bank account.

#### Accommodation

- **Arrangements:** You are responsible for booking your accommodation, but Mitacs provides a dedicated **housing stipend**.
    
- **Options:** On-campus university dorms, sublets from local students, or off-campus shared housing.
    
- **Support:** Globalink Student Mentors assist with recommendations and housing listings before arrival.

#### Visa

- **Visa Type:** Short-term **Visitor Visa / Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA)** paired with a **Work Permit Exemption** (under section R186(a) for public research interns).
    
- **Mitacs Assistance:** Mitacs provides an official Letter of Award (LOA) and an employer compliance exemption number, which streamlines the visa approval process.

#### Acceptance rate

- **Selectivity:** Highly competitive. The global acceptance rate is roughly **10% to 15%**.
- Selection is heavily weighted on matching your specific skills to a professor's project requirements rather than raw CGPA alone.
#### Languages

- English or French.

 Standard standardized language tests (IELTS, TOEFL) are **not** required for most applicants. You demonstrate proficiency through your application essays and interviews conducted by Canadian host professors.
### Application and Nomination Details

- **Application Portal Open:** Early August to Mid-September (for the following summer).
    
- **Project Selection:** You search the Mitacs database and choose a **minimum of 3 and maximum of 10 projects** across at least **3 different Canadian provinces**.
    
- **Materials Required:**
    
    - Official Academic Transcripts (translated into English if necessary).
    
    - Standardized **Mitacs CV** detailing technical skills, tools, and research projects.
    
    - Statement of Interest (SOP highlighting research experience and interest in chosen projects).
    
- **Matching Phase:** October to December (Professors review candidates and schedule virtual interviews).

- **Offer Period:** January to February.
