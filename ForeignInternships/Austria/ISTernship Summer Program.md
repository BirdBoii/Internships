

### General Rankings

- **Global Standing:** ISTA consistently ranks among the top basic research institutions globally in normalized research output (e.g., ranked **#3 worldwide** by Nature Index normalized for institutional size).
    
- **Research Focus:** Unlike traditional comprehensive universities, ISTA is a post-graduate basic science institute modeled after Rockefeller University and Weizmann Institute. It operates exclusively on an interdisciplinary, Ph.D.-granting model.

### Main Domains

- **Computer Science & Data Science** (Algorithms, Machine Learning, Computer Graphics, Cryptography, Scientific Computing)

- **Biology & Biochemistry** (Cell Biology, Evolutionary Biology, Developmental Biology, Structural Biology)

### Details of Exchange program

#### Duration

- **Timeframe:** 2 to 3 months during the summer (typically running from **May/June to August/September**).
    
- **Presence:** Requires 100% in-person, uninterrupted full-time commitment at the ISTA campus in Klosterneuburg (near Vienna).

#### Nomination Process

- **Direct Student Application:** There is no requirement for a formal institutional nomination from your home university to apply.
    
- **Faculty Review:** Applications go through a central selection committee and are subsequently selected directly by the respective Lab Group Leaders (Professors) based on research interest matching.

#### Fees/Stipend and Expenses Management

- **Application & Tuition Fees:** **€0** (No application, tuition, or registration fees).
    
- **Monthly Stipend:** ~**€1,150/month** paid via OeAD (Austrian Agency for Education and Internationalisation) to cover local living costs.
    
- **Travel Lump Sum:** A distance-calculated travel allowance is provided to cover round-trip flights between your home country and Austria.
    
- **Health Insurance:** Handled administratively upon arrival (costs deducted from stipend or arranged via standard student health plans).

#### Accommodation

- **Options Provided:** ISTA provides guaranteed housing assistance. Interns get access to:
    
    - On-campus ISTA Guesthouse dorm rooms.
        
    - Off-campus OeAD student residences in Vienna (Heiligenstadt area).
        
- **Cost:** Approximately **€600 – €700/month** for dorm-style single rooms with shared kitchen/facilities (deducted directly or paid via the stipend).
    
- **Commute:** A dedicated ISTA Shuttle Bus runs directly between Heiligenstadt (Vienna subway terminal) and the campus (free for ISTerns).


#### Visa

- **Visa Type:** Requires an Austrian **Visum D (Special Cases of Gainful Employment / Researcher Visa)** or a Residence Permit depending on country of origin and total duration.
    
- **Administrative Support:** ISTA and OeAD issue an official hosting agreement and scholarship confirmation letter, satisfying all proof-of-funds and accommodation requirements for European embassy processing.
    
- **Travel Insurance:** Applicants must arrange short-term travel insurance (10–14 days) prior to travel to cover visa validity before local setup takes effect.

#### Acceptance rate

**Selectivity:** Highly competitive, with an estimated acceptance rate of **<2%**

**Cohort Size:** Receives several thousand applications globally every year for roughly **40–50 fellowship spots**.
#### Languages

**100% English** across all laboratories, research groups, coursework, and campus administration.

No formal English language test certificates (TOEFL/IELTS) are mandatory; proficiency is evaluated through your Statement of Purpose, academic history, and interview.
### Application and Nomination Details

- **Eligibility:** Open to enrolled Bachelor’s or Master’s students (or recent graduates) who have completed at least 4 semesters of university prior to starting.
    
- **Portal & Timeline:** Applied through the centralized ISTA online portal. Applications typically open in **December** and close by **early February** annually.
    
- **Required Documents:**
    
    1. Updated CV (ISTA provides a recommended template).
        
    2. Statement of Purpose (SOP) detailing motivation, specific research group interest, and career goals.
        
    3. Official Academic Transcripts.
