### Details of Exchange program

#### Duration

- **Standard Length:** Typically 8 to 12 weeks (2 to 3 months) during the summer period (May – August).
    
- **Flexibility:** Exact dates are determined mutually between you and your host professor.

#### Nomination Process

- **No Central Nomination Portal:** Unlike formal semester exchanges, there is no official central university nomination or application portal for visiting summer undergrads.
    
- **Faculty Sponsorship:** Your "nomination" is simply an agreement by a TU Delft faculty member/PI to host you. Once a professor agrees, their faculty HR/International Office handles the internal guest registration.

#### Fees/Stipend and Expenses Management

- **Stipend (Professor Budget):**
    
    - If the lab has active grant funding (e.g., EU projects or industrial contracts), the professor can offer a monthly stipend, typically **€800 to €1,200/month**.
        
    - If the lab lacks flexible short-term funding, the position will be unfunded (self-sponsored).
        
- **Living Costs:** Total monthly expenses in Delft range between **€1,000 and €1,300/month** (housing, food, transport, mandatory health insurance).

#### Accommodation

- **University Housing (DUWO):** TU Delft works with the student housing organization DUWO. However, short-stay summer housing through DUWO is extremely limited and prioritized for full-degree or semester exchange students.
    
- **Alternative Options:** Visiting interns often rely on private platforms (Kamernet, HousingAnywhere) or subletting rooms from regular TU Delft students who go abroad for summer break.
    
- **Cost:** Expect room rents to range from **€500 to €850/month**.

#### Visa

- **Schengen Short-Stay Visa (Type C):** Required for stays under 90 days for Indian passport holders.
    
- **Purpose:** Business / Research Internship.
    
- **Supporting Documents:** The host department at TU Delft provides an official **Invitation Letter / Guest Agreement** stating the duration and nature of your research, which serves as the primary document for your VFS Netherlands visa application.

#### Acceptance rate

**Cold Outreach Conversion Rate:** Typically **2% to 5%**. Professors receive high volumes of cold emails; selection depends strictly on lab capacity, your academic match, CGPA, and funding availability in that specific lab.
#### Languages

**English**

### Application and Nomination Details

- **How to Apply:** Direct cold outreach to professors/PIs via email between September and November.
    
- **Key Materials Needed:**
    
    1. Highly tailored email matching your background to 1–2 of their specific papers.
        
    2. Concise Academic CV (1–2 pages) emphasizing programming skills (MATLAB, Python, C++), project work, and IIT CGPA.
        
    3. Current academic transcript.
        
    4. Brief outline of your availability (exact start/end dates).
