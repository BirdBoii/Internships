#### Stipend  and Expenses Management- 

3,486CHF per month tax free and Health coverage. 2.5 Days paid leave per month and 2 weeks before closing. More benefits for married people/ people with children.

Travel expenses will be partly covered to Geneva depending on individual circumstances.
#### Accommodation

They don't directly provide accommodation, but they have an internal database through which they will help students in finding apartments. Initially you can book rooms in their hostel while you figure out your housing situation.

#### Visa

- **Invitation & Official Status:** Once accepted, CERN provides official contractual documentation recognized by Swiss and French authorities.
    
- **Special Cards (Carte de Légitimation / Titre de Séjour):** Upon arrival, CERN’s HR department assists you in getting international administrative status cards (issued by the Swiss Federal Department of Foreign Affairs and/or the French Ministry of Foreign Affairs). These cards allow you to reside legally and cross the Swiss-French border seamlessly for work.
    
- **Visa Processing:** If you are a non-EU/EEA passport holder (or require an entry visa to enter Switzerland/France), CERN issues formal visa support letters to accelerate your appointment at the embassy.
