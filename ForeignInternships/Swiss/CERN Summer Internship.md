### Details of Exchange program

#### Duration

8-13 Weeks (June to August)

#### Nomination Process

CV, academic transcripts, and at least one reference letter

You have to had completed at least 3 years before starting the internship. You can also apply after completing UG Provided that the semester is completed just before joining.

#### Fees/Stipend and Expenses Management

You receive a daily subsistence allowance of **~90 to 94 CHF per day** (which translates to roughly 2,700+ CHF per month).

CERN reimburses your round-trip travel to Geneva and provides full comprehensive health insurance for the duration of your stay.

#### Accommodation

CERN helps you find housing. Most summer students are placed in the CERN hostel on-site or in nearby rented apartments, and you pay your rent out of your daily stipend.

#### Visa

If you need a visa for Switzerland/France, CERN sends an official invitation letter around May to expedite your visa process at the embassy.
