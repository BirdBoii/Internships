### Details of Exchange program

#### Duration

Unlike university programs that have a single, rigid summer cohort, **DLR institutes take interns year-round on a project-by-project basis**.

Usually 2 to 3 months (minimum 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the lab and project scope).


#### Nomination Process

##### Route A: DAAD WISE Funding (Recommended)

This is the smoothest route for Indian B.Tech students.

1. Reach out directly to a DLR research group leader or PhD researcher starting around **August–September** of your 3rd year.
    
2. Explain that you want to conduct summer research at their lab and will be applying for funding via **DAAD WISE**.
    
3. Once they agree, they will issue you an **Invitation Letter**.
    
4. Submit your DAAD WISE application by **November 1**. If selected, DAAD covers your monthly stipend, travel, and health insurance.

##### Route B: Direct Application via DLR Careers Portal

If you do not get DAAD WISE funding or miss the deadline, you can apply directly to DLR:

1. **Advertised Positions:** Go to the [DLR Careers Portal](https://www.dlr.de/en/careers) and filter by **Category: Praktikum / Internship** and your domain (e.g., _Aeronautics_ or _Space_). Apply to listed openings.
    
2. **Speculative Applications (_Initiativbewerbung_):** Many institutes do not post every single internship opening online. Most DLR institute pages (e.g., Institute of Aerodynamics, Institute of Flight Systems) have a contact form or direct email for speculative internship applications.
#### Fees/Stipend and Expenses Management

€500 to €900+ per month, depends on the contract type

> **Pro-Tip:** During your interview or initial email exchange with the DLR supervisor, it is standard practice to politely ask:
> 
> _"Is this internship position funded through a paid contract (Vergütung), or would I need to arrange independent funding?"_



#### Accommodation

- **Housing:** DLR sites like Oberpfaffenhofen or Braunschweig often have local student housing networks or internal pinboards where departing researchers/interns sublet their rooms to incoming interns.

#### Visa

- **ZAV Approval (Work Authorization):** For non-EU students doing an internship in Germany, the Federal Employment Agency (_Bundesagentur für Arbeit_) must issue a ZAV certificate. DLR's Human Resources (HR) department will file this paperwork on your behalf once you sign the agreement.
    
- **Visa Application:** With your DLR contract/invitation and ZAV approval (or DAAD award letter), you will apply for a **German Internship Visa** through VFS Global in India.
