### Details of Exchange program

#### Duration

A typical internship lasts 12 to 14 weeks, but longer internships are possible.
Internships can be completed at any time of the year, but most interns start during the summer months. We process applications for internships starting in the summer months and those starting outside the summer months differently.

#### Nomination Process

The candidate should have completed the first three years of a standard Computer Science Bachelors program _by the start of the internship_. People with a similar background can also apply for the internships.
- Applicants with interdisciplinary interests in Computer Science and another area, e.g. intersecting social science, economics, and law, may apply. However, the primary focus of internships will be applications of these areas to Computer Science.
- Our internships are very competitive and all applicants are expected to have an excellent academic record, regardless of its length.

#### Fees/Stipend and Expenses Management

All internships are fully funded, covering living costs, housing and roundtrip travel costs.

### Application and Nomination Details

**For internships starting during the summer** (i.e., starting between May and August), we have a strict admissions process. **The deadline for applying for a summer internship** is November 1 of the previous year. If you apply for this deadline and your application is successful, you should hear from us before 1st of February. If you have not heard back by this date, please assume that you were not selected.

**For internships starting outside the summer** (i.e., starting between January and April or between September and December), we do not have a fixed deadline and applications are examined based on the availability of projects and the needs of research groups. Apply at least 5 months before your intended starting date. If you do not hear back within 4 months of applying, please assume that you were not selected.

Submit applications here - https://apply.cis.mpg.de/register/internship
